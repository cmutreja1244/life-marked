# Physical QR checklist

Canonical engraved URL (never change the path):

`https://lifemarked.co.uk/q/{public_token}`

- High error correction, 4-module quiet zone, black on white.
- No centre LifeMarked mark until engraving tests pass.
- Download SVG, 2048 PNG, and labelled print PDF from admin.
- Scan with iOS Camera and Android Chrome on a cemetery-like outdoor network.
- Confirm one hop to the memorial (no intermediate marketing page).
- Confirm `www.lifemarked.co.uk/q/{token}` 301s to apex and still renders the same person.
- Change the human slug; confirm the QR payload is unchanged.
- Disable the memorial: QR still resolves to the unavailable page.
- Do not engrave a second host.
